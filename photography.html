my approach.

I'm an observer, a candid-documentary-style photographer, shooting 35mm film & digital. My approach focuses on capturing the unposed, natural, & intuitive moments. Photography allows me not only to catch moments in time but also to collect blips of sweet memories, photographing what is & holding on to what once was; it's the memorabilia of photos & the memories tied to them that make photography magical.
