about me

Hello! I’m Sophie, a Montreal-based creative.

growing up, art class was always my favourite. In summer camp, I advocated for arts & crafts over competitive sports (having to participate in sports made me cry). I’ve always been a daydreamer and have felt most like myself when I'm exercising my imagination.

I’m always learning and creating; it’s been a lifelong loop.

I started to notice the magic in my creativity, which led me to pursue my curiosity in photography and graphic design, & my devotion to living a creative life. It felt intuitive to (finally) merge work and play. Think of me as a creative boutique & your creative partner; let’s create something with hearts in it.

warmly, Sophie
